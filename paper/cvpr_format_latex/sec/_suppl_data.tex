All evaluation is performed on held-out test cases only. For each task the model
is trained on a single source contrast and evaluated on every contrast of that
task's test split, including the training contrast itself; training and
validation subjects never appear in any reported number.

\paragraph{ON-Harmony} \cite{warrington2025onharmony} --- 31-region healthy-brain
segmentation. Reference labels are \emph{silver standard}: FreeSurfer
parcellations~\cite{fischl2012freesurfer} of each session's T1w, grouped into 31
regions and rigidly registered with ANTs into the native space of the same
session's other contrasts. They are
not manual annotations, so absolute Dice here is agreement with an automated
reference; every method is scored against the same reference. The test set is
eight sessions of four subjects, each scanned at two sites.

\paragraph{Open-MS} \cite{lesjak2018openms} --- MS-lesion segmentation with
manual consensus annotations, evaluated across FLAIR, T1w and T2w.

\paragraph{Brats2024-GLI} \cite{deverdier2024brats2024} --- post-treatment
glioma sub-region segmentation (necrotic core, surrounding FLAIR hyperintensity,
enhancing tumor, resection cavity), trained on each of T1n, T1c, T2w and
T2-FLAIR and evaluated on all four.

\paragraph{CHAOS} \cite{kavur2021chaos} --- abdominal organ segmentation (liver,
spleen, both kidneys), evaluated on the CHAOS MR contrasts (four held-out MR
patients) and CT (20 held-out patients), and on two external cohorts: CT and
T2w MRI from AMOS~\cite{ji2022amos} and liver-only CT from
SLIVER07~\cite{heimann2009comparison}. In the pooled CT group, liver comes from
CHAOS, AMOS and SLIVER07 and spleen and kidneys from CHAOS and AMOS. CHAOS
images a restricted axial field of view, so the full-torso external volumes are
cropped to the CHAOS-equivalent axial slab (median CHAOS margins around the
kidneys for AMOS and the liver for SLIVER07) before prediction; the network
sees, and is scored on, only the cropped volume.

\paragraph{ToothFairy2, HaN-Seg and PDDCA}
\cite{bolelli2025toothfairy2,podobnik2023hanseg,raudaschl2017pddca} ---
mandible segmentation, trained on dental cone-beam CT, the one non-CT, non-MRI
training modality here. ToothFairy2's annotation coverage is
cohort-dependent, so every column of this task is scored on the mandible alone
(teeth excluded, matching the external references), which keeps the numbers
being averaged commensurable. ToothFairy2 acquires CBCT only, so apart from its
own 71 CBCT test cases its held-out contrasts all come from other datasets:
HaN-Seg CT and MR-T1 (42 cases each) and PDDCA CT (40 cases), the two CT sources
pooled as one contrast group. ToothFairy2's volumes
are stored superior-inferior inverted relative to the external cohorts --- an
inversion invisible to the image header, found by checking anatomy directly ---
so the external test volumes are flipped into ToothFairy2's convention before
inference, which leaves Dice and HD95 unchanged under the rigid transform. The
correction matters for the comparison: a method without flip augmentation
(SRCSM) was penalised by the inversion far more than the others, and it is
after this correction that SRCSM leads this task.

\paragraph{Breast: I-SPY2, Duke-Breast-MRI, I-SPY1, ACRIN-6698}
\cite{li2022ispy2,saha2018duke,garrucho2025mamamia,hylton2012ispy1,partridge2018acrin6698}
--- breast-lesion segmentation on malignant-only cohorts. I-SPY2 (CC BY 4.0)
trains, on its first-post-contrast T1 (T1-CE) and its T2w; its reference is the
trial's functional tumor volume mask. Three external cohorts are test-only.
Duke-Breast-MRI and I-SPY1 come through the MAMA-MIA release, whose expert
primary-lesion protocol both share, and contribute T1-CE and pre-contrast
columns; Duke-Breast-MRI is CC BY-NC 4.0, a non-commercial term no other cohort
here carries. ACRIN-6698, the diffusion sub-study of I-SPY2 with no patient in
common with our I-SPY2 subjects, contributes the baseline diffusion-weighted
acquisition with the trial's whole-tumor DWI region, a contrast neither
training modality resembles. Because I-SPY2 is mostly acquired unilaterally,
bilateral external volumes are cropped to the lesion side and, for the
full-thorax acquisitions, also anterior-posteriorly to the breast-plus-chest-wall
extent of I-SPY2's own crops; without the second crop every method's HD95 is
dominated by the thorax. I-SPY2's DCE series are acquired unilaterally at some
sites and bilaterally at others; both field-of-view variants are kept per
patient, and the significance tests treat them as one patient.

\paragraph{Spine} \cite{vandergraaf2024spider} --- vertebrae, intervertebral
discs and spinal canal. Results for this task were computed on a separate pipeline with the same
method suite except SRCSM, trained on CT
and Dixon in-phase MRI of a CT/Dixon cohort and tested on that cohort's held-out
CT and Dixon fat, in-phase and water images and on the T1w and T2w of SPIDER.
Dice and HD95 are computed on each whole structure per label (the
pipeline's \texttt{global\_bin} metrics). No SRCSM arm was run, so Spine
is absent from every SRCSM comparison.

\paragraph{Pelvis} \cite{wasserthal2023totalseg,dantonoli2025totalsegmri} ---
eleven pelvic and hip musculoskeletal structures (both hips, sacrum, and the
gluteus maximus, medius and minimus and iliopsoas on each side), from the
TotalSegmentator CT (CC BY 4.0) and MRI (CC BY-NC-SA 2.0) releases. The two are
unpaired cohorts, and the MRI release mixes sequences by design, so its MRI
column is a sequence-heterogeneous test rather than one named contrast. CT is
subsampled to 200 cases to keep training time comparable with the other tasks.
