All evaluation is performed on held-out test cases only. Each model is trained
on one modality of its task and evaluated on every test contrast of that task,
the training contrast included; training and
validation subjects never appear in any reported number.

\paragraph{Glioma (BraTS2024-GLI~\cite{deverdier2024brats2024}).} Post-treatment
glioma sub-region segmentation (necrotic core, surrounding non-enhancing FLAIR
hyperintensity (SNFH), enhancing tumor, resection cavity), trained on each of
T1n, T1c, T2w and FLAIR and evaluated on all four.

\paragraph{MS (Open-MS~\cite{lesjak2018openms}).} MS-lesion segmentation with
manual consensus annotations, evaluated across FLAIR, T1w and T2w.

\paragraph{Breast (I-SPY2; external: Duke, I-SPY1, ACRIN-6698~\cite{li2022ispy2,saha2018duke,garrucho2025mamamia,hylton2012ispy1,partridge2018acrin6698}).}
Breast-lesion segmentation on malignant-only cohorts. I-SPY2
trains, on its first-post-contrast T1 (T1-CE) and its T2w; its reference is the
trial's functional tumor volume mask. Three external cohorts are test-only.
Duke and I-SPY1 come through the MAMA-MIA release, whose expert
primary-lesion protocol both share, and contribute T1-CE and pre-contrast
columns. ACRIN-6698, the diffusion sub-study of I-SPY2 with no patient in
common with our I-SPY2 subjects, contributes the baseline diffusion-weighted
acquisition with the trial's whole-tumor DWI region, a contrast neither
training modality resembles. Because I-SPY2 is mostly acquired unilaterally,
bilateral external volumes are cropped to the lesion side and, for the
full-thorax acquisitions, also anterior-posteriorly to the breast-plus-chest-wall
extent of I-SPY2's own crops; without the second crop every method's HD95 is
dominated by the thorax. I-SPY2's DCE series are acquired unilaterally at some
sites and bilaterally at others; both field-of-view variants are kept per
patient, and the significance tests treat them as one patient.

\paragraph{Abdomen (CHAOS~\cite{kavur2021chaos}; external: AMOS~\cite{ji2022amos}, SLIVER07~\cite{heimann2009comparison}).}
Abdominal organ segmentation (liver, spleen, both kidneys), evaluated on the
CHAOS MR contrasts (four held-out patients) and CT (20 held-out patients), and
on two external cohorts: CT ($100$) and T2w MRI ($20$) from AMOS and liver-only
CT ($20$) from SLIVER07. In the pooled CT group, liver comes from
CHAOS, AMOS and SLIVER07 and spleen and kidneys from AMOS only (CHAOS CT is
annotated for the liver only). The full-torso external volumes are cropped
to the axial field of view of CHAOS before prediction (field-of-view matching,
below).

\paragraph{Brain (ON-Harmony~\cite{warrington2025onharmony}).} 31-region healthy-brain
segmentation. Reference labels are \emph{silver standard}: FreeSurfer
parcellations~\cite{fischl2012freesurfer} of each session's T1w, grouped into 31
regions and rigidly registered with ANTs into the native space of the same
session's other contrasts. They are
not manual annotations, so absolute Dice here is agreement with an automated
reference; every method is scored against the same reference. The test set is
eight sessions of four subjects, each scanned at two sites.

\paragraph{Mandible (ToothFairy2; external: HaN-Seg, PDDCA~\cite{bolelli2025toothfairy2,podobnik2023hanseg,raudaschl2017pddca}).}
Mandible segmentation, trained on dental cone-beam CT, the one non-CT, non-MRI
training modality here. ToothFairy2's annotation coverage is
cohort-dependent, so every column of this task is scored on the mandible alone
(teeth excluded, matching the external references), which keeps the numbers
being averaged commensurable. ToothFairy2 acquires CBCT only, so apart from its
own 71 CBCT test cases its held-out contrasts all come from other datasets:
HaN-Seg CT ($42$ cases) and MR-T1 ($41$) and PDDCA CT ($40$), the two CT sources
pooled as one contrast group. ToothFairy2's volumes
are stored superior-inferior inverted relative to the external cohorts --- an
inversion invisible to the image header, found by checking anatomy directly ---
so the external test volumes are flipped into ToothFairy2's convention before
inference, which leaves Dice and HD95 unchanged under the rigid transform. The
correction matters for the comparison: a method without flip augmentation
(SRCSM) was penalized by the inversion far more than the others, and it is
after this correction that SRCSM leads this task.

\paragraph{Spine (CT/Dixon cohort; external: SPIDER~\cite{vandergraaf2024spider}).} Vertebrae, intervertebral
discs and spinal canal. Results for this task were computed on a separate pipeline with the same
method suite except SRCSM, trained on CT
and Dixon in-phase MRI of a CT/Dixon cohort and tested on that cohort's held-out
CT and Dixon fat, in-phase and water images and on the T1w and T2w of SPIDER.
Dice and HD95 are computed on each whole structure per label. No SRCSM arm was run, so Spine
is absent from every SRCSM comparison.

\paragraph{Pelvis (TotalSegmentator~\cite{wasserthal2023totalseg,dantonoli2025totalsegmri}).}
Eleven pelvic and hip musculoskeletal structures (both hips, sacrum, and the
gluteus maximus, medius and minimus and iliopsoas on each side), from the
TotalSegmentator CT and MRI releases. The two are
unpaired cohorts, and the MRI release mixes sequences by design, so its MRI
column is a sequence-heterogeneous test rather than one named contrast. CT is
subsampled to 200 cases to keep training time comparable with the other tasks.

\paragraph{Field-of-view matching.} A model tested on a field of view it never
saw in training can fail for reasons that have nothing to do with contrast or
modality. Where an external cohort images more of the body than the training
cohort, we therefore crop its volumes before prediction, so that the shift
left between training and test is, as far as possible, the contrast or
modality alone. Abdomen (AMOS, SLIVER07): the axial slab CHAOS covers, placed
with the median CHAOS margins around the kidneys (AMOS) or the liver
(SLIVER07). Mandible (HaN-Seg, PDDCA): a $144\times128\times80$\,mm box centered
on the mandible, close to the 90th percentile of ToothFairy2's training
extent, enlarged (at most to $154\times154\times89$\,mm) only when needed to
contain the whole mandible. Breast (Duke, ACRIN-6698): the lesion-side
half of bilateral volumes, then, for full-thorax acquisitions, an
anterior-posterior window from $27$\,mm in front of the skin to $147$\,mm
behind it, the measured geometry of I-SPY2's unilateral acquisitions, extended
if needed to contain the lesion; I-SPY1 is natively unilateral and is not
cropped. The network sees, and is scored on, only the cropped volume. Each crop
is placed using the reference annotation (organ, mandible or lesion position).
This reveals the target's position, not its extent, and is identical for every
method, so it does not favor any method; but absolute Dice and HD95 are better
than a pipeline that had to localize the target first would obtain.
